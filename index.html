Privacy policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026
Developer: Arunkumar

Creator OS works offline and does not require an account. The app does not send your workspace content to the developer and does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Information stored on your Mac

Creator OS stores the ideas, drafts, projects, tasks, references, imported attachments, templates, publication records, and preferences you create or choose to import. It also stores local draft recovery information and document revisions to help protect your work. This information is used on your Mac to provide the app’s features.

Collection and sharing

The app does not collect personal information for the developer, sell data, track you across apps or websites, or automatically publish to social platforms. Creator OS does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

Files, exports, and backups

You choose which files to import and where to save exports and portable backups. Optional automatic backups are stored locally. Exported documents and backups can contain your workspace content; anyone you share those files with may be able to read them. If you save files in a folder synchronized by another provider, such as a cloud drive, that provider handles those files under its own policies. Creator OS does not control operating-system or third-party backups.

Local system information

The app stores preferences, reads timestamps to manage its own automatic backups, and checks available disk space before writing a backup. These operations support local functionality and are not used to identify or track you.

External links and Apple services

When you choose to open a web reference, the privacy policy, or an email link, the destination website, browser, or email provider handles that interaction under its own privacy practices. Apple separately handles App Store downloads, purchases where applicable, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

Workspace data remains on your Mac until you remove it. Archiving an item keeps it recoverable and does not erase it. Removing the app may leave local application data, exports, or backups behind. You control exported files and external backups and should remove those copies separately if you no longer need them. Contact support if you need help locating and removing local data.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include and uses it to respond to your request and resolve the issue. The email provider processes the message. Please avoid sending passwords, sensitive drafts, or full workspace backups unless needed and specifically requested. You may request deletion of support correspondence; information may be retained where necessary to meet legal obligations or resolve an ongoing issue.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a support message, contact the developer to request deletion.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s practices change. The current policy and its effective date will be published on this page.
